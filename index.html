A little about me

I spent 35 years teaching English in government schools, retiring as an English School Assistant in 2017. Along the way I served as a District Resource Person and coordinated the District English Mobile Team — always looking for new ways to make grammar feel less like a rulebook and more like a game.

After retiring, I was honoured with the District Best Teacher Award, worked as a Panel Inspector observing English classrooms, and I currently teach English to children, students, employees, job seekers, and learning enthusiasts. I even composed songs to help children remember English poems!

These lessons are the same ones I've taught with chalk and notebook for decades — now written down for any child to learn, click by click...
